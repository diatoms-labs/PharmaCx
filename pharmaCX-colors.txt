## TEXT ON COLORED BACKGROUNDS
# ------------------------------
# On Navy (#0F3D6E)      → use White #FFFFFF or Accent #4FB8F5
# On Primary (#1E7FC4)   → use White #FFFFFF
# On Accent (#4FB8F5)    → use Navy #0F3D6E (dark text)
# On Tint (#E8F4FB)      → use Navy #0F3D6E
# On Green (#1B6B3A)     → use White #FFFFFF
# On White / Light bg    → use Navy #0F3D6E for brand text
